Einstein's garden

Tucked behind the famous summerhouse lies Einstein’s Garden — a quiet patch of green where the world’s most famous thinker found calm among cabbages, cosmos, and cosmic thoughts. Einstein believed that nature was the best teacher — and that a walk among plants could solve almost any problem (except maybe quantum mechanics). The garden isn’t huge, but its roots run deep: this is where simplicity met genius, where time slowed down, and where relativity took a breather.
